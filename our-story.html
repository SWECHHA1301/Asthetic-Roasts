Chapter I

Ethical Origins

Our journey begins in the high-altitude cloud forests of Ethiopia and Colombia.
We don't just buy beans; we cultivate relationships. Every harvest is traceable
back to the single lot, ensuring that the hands that picked the cherries are as
respected as the hands that brew the cup.

Direct Trade, Zero Compromise.

We pay 40% above Fair Trade minimums because we believe that sustainability is not a marketing
term—it's the only way forward. By investing in the soil and the souls of our farmers, we ensure the
future of the ritual.
